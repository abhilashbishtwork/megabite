This policy applies to orders placed on the MegaBite app.

Your money is safe

If money was deducted from your UPI, card, net-banking or wallet but your order wasn't confirmed, don't worry. It is protected under consumer safeguarding guidelines and will be either processed or refunded.

Cancellations

We start cooking as soon as your order is confirmed and dispatch a delivery partner right away, so orders cannot be cancelled once placed. Please double-check your items and address before you pay.

In exceptional cases, such as an item being unavailable or an unforeseen emergency at our end, we may cancel the order and refund you in full. Other exceptions are reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed.

Failed or pending payments

  • This usually happens when the bank can't confirm the payment immediately.
  • If the bank confirms within 15 minutes, your order is processed automatically.
  • If the bank confirms after 15 minutes, the order is rejected and a refund is started automatically.

Missing, wrong or damaged items

If something is missing, incorrect or not up to the mark, raise it from the order in the app's Help section or write to us within 24 hours with your order ID and a photo. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method.

Refund timelines

Once a refund is started, it usually takes 5–7 working days to reach your account, depending on your bank.
