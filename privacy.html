MegaBite is a brand of Curefoods India Private Limited (“MegaBite”, “we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you visit this website or use the MegaBite app (together, the “Services”). It is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

By using the Services you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Information we collect

When you use the MegaBite app we collect only what we need to take and deliver your order:

  • Your name and mobile number, used to create and verify your account.
  • Delivery address and, if you allow it, your device location, used to find your nearest kitchen and deliver your order.
  • Email address, date of birth and anniversary, only if you choose to share them.
  • Order history, preferences and support conversations.
  • Basic device and usage information such as device type, app version, IP address and crash logs.

Payments are processed by licensed payment gateways. We do not store your full card, UPI or net-banking credentials.

This website itself does not ask you for any personal information.

How we use it

  • Verify your account and process, prepare and deliver your orders.
  • Send order status updates by SMS, WhatsApp, call or app notification.
  • Provide customer support and resolve refunds or complaints.
  • Share offers and birthday or anniversary treats, which you can opt out of at any time.
  • Improve our menu, app and service, and prevent fraud or misuse.

Sharing

We never sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With our group companies, delivery partners, payment gateways and technology providers (including our ordering platform provider, which hosts account data on secure servers) strictly to run the Services, under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, court order or a government authority, or to prevent fraud, cyber incidents or other offences.

Cookies

This website may use basic cookies and analytics to understand traffic and improve the site. Cookies cannot read data from your device and you can disable them in your browser settings.

Data security & retention

We use reasonable security practices, including encryption, access controls and firewalled servers, to protect your information. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe and retain it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can access or update your profile in the app, opt out of promotional messages, or ask us to delete your account by writing to megabite@curefoods.in.

Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date it was last updated.
